MIN: 5

TITLE: THE OFFER
MIN: 6
@@SLATE
Camera :: The equipment truck, empty, from the doorway — Dutch and Leo at opposite ends of a long steel box.
Roll :: Digital, 1250 ISO, one fluorescent, the doors open onto a grey car park.
Costume :: Dutch in her good coat. Leo with the cooler bag he is embarrassed to be carrying.
Environment :: The truck on the last morning: shelves bare, labels left behind, a broom nobody has used.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. EQUIPMENT TRUCK — MORNING</h4>
      <p class="action">Dutch is writing in a notebook. Leo stands. She does not look up.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DUTCH</p><p class="dline">Next show is in March. Six months, three states, good rate. I need a best boy who can run a truck and doesn't panic.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">That's a job offer.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DUTCH</p><p class="dline">It's a job offer.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">I'm twenty.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DUTCH</p><p class="dline">I was nineteen when I did my first show. I slept in the truck. It's not a reason.</p></div>
    </div>
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">EXT. EQUIPMENT TRUCK — LATER</h4>
      <p class="action">Car park. Dutch smokes. Leo doesn't.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DUTCH</p><p class="dline">Here's the thing nobody tells you at twenty. If you go, you'll be good. You'll be very good in about four years. And you'll come back here at thirty and the store will be a phone repair shop and you'll feel nothing, which will frighten you.</p></div>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DUTCH</p><p class="dline">No. It's a decision. Those don't come with answers, they come with a date.</p></div>

    </div>

TITLE: THE COUNTER
MIN: 5
@@SLATE
Camera :: The store from outside, through the glass, at 5:55 a.m. — the frame waits for the lights to come on.
Roll :: Digital, 1600 ISO, blue exterior, then the tubes flickering to life one bank at a time.
Costume :: Leo in the apron again, over the crew hoodie. Sam in a coat with a flask.
Environment :: Kessler Hardware before opening: four hundred bulbs, no customers, a radio playing something from 1987.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. KESSLER HARDWARE — DAWN</h4>
      <p class="action">Leo turns the lights on bank by bank. Sam watches from the door with two coffees.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SAM</p><p class="dline">You're early.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">You're earlier.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SAM</p><p class="dline">I'm the owner. I'm allowed to be sentimental about a building.</p></div>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SAM</p><p class="dline">So. What did it feel like?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">Like being useful for eleven weeks and then stopping.</p></div>

    </div>
<div class="scene">

TITLE: STOCK COUNT
MIN: 6
@@SLATE

Roll :: Digital, 1000 ISO, closing time, every bank on, the store lit for an audit.
Costume :: Sam in shirtsleeves, no cardigan, which is how you know. Leo in the apron. An ACCOUNTANT with a tablet.
Environment :: Every aisle at once: four hundred bulbs, forty drawers, and a back room that has not been swept since 1994.

<div class="scene">

TITLE: THE LETTER
