TITLE: THE FLOOD
MIN: 6
@@SLATE
Camera :: Handheld in rain, then a wide of a river where a road used to be — the camera gets wet and does not complain, because it is being paid.
Roll :: Digital, 3200 ISO, grey light and headlights; rain on the lens element held there and wiped with a chamois every eleven seconds.
Costume :: RUTH-ANNE COBB in a hi-vis jacket with EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT on the back. June in a rain jacket over the fleece.
Environment :: The county road at the river: water over the tarmac, a submerged sign, a truck parked too close, a man in a boat.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">EXT. COUNTY ROAD — RIVER CROSSING — MORNING</h4>
      <p class="action">Water where the road was. A stop sign with only its top half above the surface. RUTH-ANNE COBB, fifty-eight, stands in the rain with a clipboard she has given up on and a phone in a plastic bag.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">RUTH-ANNE</p><p class="dline">Get the waterline on the Ellis place and then get the culvert. The county needs pictures or the state doesn't send money.</p></div>

    </div>
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">EXT. ELLIS PLACE — PORCH — LATER</h4>

<div class="scene">
