TITLE: THE EDIT BAY
MIN: 5
@@SLATE
Camera :: Over Leo's shoulder at the laptop, the timeline glowing; then a slow push in until the footage is bigger than the room.
Roll :: Digital, 640 ISO, one desk lamp and a screen.
Costume :: Both of them in yesterday's clothes, which is how you know it's the edit.
Environment :: Maya's bedroom turned edit bay: laptop, headphones, two mugs, a fan, the door open for air.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. MAYA'S BEDROOM — NIGHT</h4>
      <p class="action">The timeline: eleven minutes and forty seconds of colour bars and clips with names like TAKE63_USE and TRUCK_GOOD.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">The first cut is four hours long.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">It's nineteen minutes.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">I mean it feels like four hours. Everything's in it because I couldn't decide.</p></div>
      <p class="action">Maya takes the headphones. Watches. Her face does nothing, which is worst.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">Cut the first two minutes.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">That's the setup.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">Cut it. Start with the crying and let them catch up. If they can't catch up, we've made a film for people who need the setup and that's a smaller audience.</p></div>
    </div>
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. MAYA'S BEDROOM — LATER</h4>
      <p class="action">Deshawn's face, full screen. One tear. The room is silent except the fan.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">This is a real film, isn't it.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">Don't say that yet.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">Why not?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">Because it's the kind of thing you say at the good part, and then the good part is over and you've spent the sentence.</p></div>
      <p class="action">They watch it again. And again. The fan turns. Outside, a dog barks at nothing for a full minute.</p>
    </div>

<div class="scene">

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">This is twelve minutes long. It's called Coochie. It's about a word. Please laugh at the parts that are funny and don't laugh at the parts that aren't.</p></div>

TITLE: THE LAUGH
MIN: 6
@@SLATE
Camera :: The audience from the front — the film's one reverse shot of its own screening.
Roll :: Digital, 3200 ISO, faces lit by the sheet.
Costume :: The room.
Environment :: The basement during the Q&A; a microphone that doesn't work.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. CHURCH BASEMENT — NIGHT</h4>
      <p class="action">Q&amp;A. A man in the back row, fifty, stands with his arms folded.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAN</p><p class="dline">I've got a question. Why that word?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">Because it was used on us and we wanted it back.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAN</p><p class="dline">Used on you.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">Yes.</p></div>
      <p class="action">The man laughs. Not a cruel laugh — an uncomfortable one, the kind that arrives before a thought does.</p>
      <p class="action">The room hears it. Maya hears it. Leo's hand goes still on the laptop.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">That. That's the laugh. That's the whole reason the film exists.</p></div>
      <p class="action">The man stops laughing. Sits down. The room is silent in a different way than before.</p>
    </div>
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. CHURCH BASEMENT — LATER</h4>
      <p class="action">People leaving. The hot dogs are gone. Priya comes up with her notebook closed.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">PRIYA</p><p class="dline">The crying scene is real. The party scene is better than the crying scene, which is a problem, because you think the crying scene is the film.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">That's the most useful thing anyone's said to me all summer.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">PRIYA</p><p class="dline">Don't get used to it.</p></div>
      <p class="action">She goes. Maya stands alone in the middle of the room with a tray of empty bun wrappers.</p>
    </div>
