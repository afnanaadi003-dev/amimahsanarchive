TITLE: REYES MOTORS
MIN: 7
@@SLATE
Camera :: A slow dolly across a car lot at golden hour, past six cars and a banner, to a girl standing where the banner meets the asphalt.
Roll :: Digital, 800 ISO, actual sunset, which is the only free light in the county.
Costume :: Dolly in the blazer and sneakers, because she came from work. Ray in the polo. A photographer in a hoodie.
Environment :: Reyes Motors: a 1978 sign with two dead letters, a showroom with a coffee machine, balloons from a sale in April still tied to a mirror.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">EXT. REYES MOTORS — LOT — DUSK</h4>
      <p class="action">The banner is up before she has signed anything: CIVIC IS HERE. Six feet tall, over the used row, with a space left for the photograph.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">You printed it already.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">RAY</p><p class="dline">It's a banner. Banners are cheap and hope is expensive.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">Dad. This is my literal name on a car lot.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">RAY</p><p class="dline">And it's my literal name over the door, and I've been standing under it for thirty-one years. Welcome to the family business. It's the same business. It was always the same business.</p></div>
      <p class="action">The photographer crouches. Dolly looks at the banner, and the light does the thing the light does at six forty in October, and she lets him take the picture.</p>
    </div>
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">EXT. REYES MOTORS — SHOWROOM DOOR — CONTINUOUS</h4>
      <p class="action">Inside, through glass, Ray's office: a desk, a phone, a photograph of Dolores-the-grandmother on the wall beside a calendar from 2019.</p>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">I didn't know that.</p></div>

    </div>

TITLE: THE PANEL
MIN: 7
@@SLATE
Camera :: From the end of a long table, three interviewers in a row and one candidate small at the far end — a composition borrowed from every bad meeting in the world.
Roll :: Digital, 1600 ISO, a room with a view of a car park and a strip light with a flicker.
Costume :: Dolly in a suit she borrowed from Tolu's sister. Three interviewers in the uniform of a company that makes flooring.
Environment :: A regional agency's boardroom: a whiteboard, a bowl of sweets, a framed award for something from 2016.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. MERIDIAN GROUP — BOARDROOM — MORNING</h4>
      <p class="action">Three interviewers. A woman with a folder, a man with a laptop, and a man who has not spoken.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">INTERVIEWER 1</p><p class="dline">Your résumé says Dolores Reyes. Your portfolio says Civic Reyes. Explain the discrepancy.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">I changed my name legally in June. The résumé is a PDF from March.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">INTERVIEWER 2</p><p class="dline">And the campaign. Was that your concept?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">The concept was mine. The execution was a team of six and a photographer who did not get paid enough.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">INTERVIEWER 1</p><p class="dline">It did two hundred thousand views.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">It did two hundred thousand views and sold eleven cars, which is four more than the month before, so it worked about as well as a balloon.</p></div>
    </div>

    </div>
