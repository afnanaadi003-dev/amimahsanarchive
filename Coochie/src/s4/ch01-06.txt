TITLE: THE FIRST AD
MIN: 6
@@SLATE
Camera :: Locked off at the back of a seminar room, then a slow push past the students' laptops to the front — the camera is a prospective client.
Roll :: Digital, 1600 ISO, classroom fluorescents and a projector throwing a slide onto a stained screen.
Costume :: Dolly in a blazer over a hoodie, the blazer three years old and clearly borrowed from an interview. Blake in a quarter-zip with a startup logo.
Environment :: Room 118, Wexler Hall: desks in a horseshoe, a whiteboard with the word POSITIONING on it three times, a window with a broken blind.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. WEXLER HALL — ROOM 118 — MORNING</h4>
      <p class="action">Eight students. A projector. DR. MIRIAM SOKOLOV, sixty-six, sits on the edge of the desk with her arms folded, which is how she listens.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SOKOLOV</p><p class="dline">Deodorant. Go.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">"Certain." It's a deodorant for people who are tired of being lied to. No ocean. No waterfall. A grey stick and a word that means you won't have to think about it again.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SOKOLOV</p><p class="dline">Who's it for?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">Everybody between twenty-five and fifty who has ever stood in a drugstore aisle holding two sticks and feeling like a failure.</p></div>
      <p class="action">Silence. BLAKE, twenty-two, in a quarter-zip, writes something down and does not look up.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SOKOLOV</p><p class="dline">What's your name?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">Dolly.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SOKOLOV</p><p class="dline">Dolly. That's a name doing two jobs already. Nobody's named Dolly and also taken seriously on the first day. You've been running that campaign your whole life. What did you do about it?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">...I got a blazer.</p></div>
      <p class="action">Sokolov laughs — one short bark — and the room exhales.</p>
    </div>
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. WEXLER HALL — ROOM 118 — CONTINUOUS</h4>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SOKOLOV</p><p class="dline">Final project. Rebrand something that cannot be rebranded. Not a product. Not a company. Something with no marketing budget and no permission.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">BLAKE</p><p class="dline">Like what.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SOKOLOV</p><p class="dline">If I tell you, you'll do that.</p></div>
      <p class="action">She writes one word on the board and underlines it twice.</p>
      <p class="action">NAMES.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SOKOLOV</p><p class="dline">A name is the first advertisement ever run on a person. It runs before you speak. It runs in every room you're not in. Bring me one that works harder than it has to.</p></div>
      <p class="action">Dolly looks at the word. Her pen is not moving. Everyone else's is.</p>
    </div>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">Dolly is a sheep and a trolley and a hand truck. Dolly is a doll that says three sentences.</p></div>

TITLE: DUAL-PURPOSE
MIN: 6
@@SLATE
Camera :: Over-the-shoulder on a laptop screen at 2 a.m., then a two-shot across the table — two people and a document, which is the shape of every argument in this series.
Roll :: Digital, 5000 ISO, screen light only, so their faces are the colour of a word processor.
Costume :: Both in hoodies now. Tolu's hair tied up with a pen through it.
Environment :: The kitchen at 2 a.m.: two mugs, a plate of crusts, a window with the laundromat's sign glowing through the blind.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. APARTMENT — KITCHEN — 2 A.M.</h4>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">Dual-purpose branding. When one asset does two jobs at once. A logo that's also a letter. A colour that's also a flag. A jingle that's also a warning.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">TOLU</p><p class="dline">And a name that's also a product.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">A name that's also a product.</p></div>
      <p class="action">She types it into the document title. TOLU reads over her shoulder for a while.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">TOLU</p><p class="dline">You're going to change your name for a class.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">I'm going to change my name because of a class.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">TOLU</p><p class="dline">Those are the same sentence.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">No. One of them means I'm doing homework. The other one means the homework is correct.</p></div>
    </div>
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. WEXLER HALL — ROOM 118 — DAY</h4>
      <p class="action">Dolly at the front of the room with three slides. Blake in the back with his arms folded.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">The strongest name in the room is the one that promises something and delivers a product. Volvo. Sharp. Caterpillar. Whole industries built on a word that was already doing another job.</p></div>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">So name myself something that means a citizen's duty and a compact sedan and see which one the world hears first.</p></div>
      <p class="action">The room goes quiet in the specific way a room goes quiet when someone has said the real thing out loud.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SOKOLOV</p><p class="dline">You understand that a brand doesn't have a mother.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">I have a mother. That's the whole problem.</p></div>
    </div>

TITLE: ROOM 214
MIN: 6
@@SLATE
Camera :: Static at the back of a small courtroom, the camera at seated height, so the bench is above everyone and the flags are the tallest things in the frame.
Roll :: Digital, 2500 ISO; fluorescent tubes and an ancient fluorescent hum.
Costume :: Dolly in the blazer and a shirt buttoned to the throat. JUDGE WARD in robes and reading glasses on a chain.
Environment :: Room 214: eleven rows of benches, four people, a flag, a water pitcher, a clock that is two minutes fast.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. COURTHOUSE — ROOM 214 — MORNING</h4>
      <p class="action">JUDGE PHYLLIS WARD, sixty-three, reads the file without looking up.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">WARD</p><p class="dline">You're asking the court to change Dolores Marie Reyes to Civic Reyes.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">Yes, Your Honour.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">WARD</p><p class="dline">First name only. No middle.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">A brand doesn't carry a middle name. It carries a promise.</p></div>
      <p class="action">Ward takes her glasses off. This is the moment the room changes temperature.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">WARD</p><p class="dline">I've had this docket for nineteen years. I've had a man change his name to his favourite footballer, a woman change hers to a colour, and a fourteen-year-old petition the court to be called Batman, which I denied. Why should this be granted?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">Because it's the only word that ever promised me anything. Volkswagen promised a people's car. Civic means the city. It means being a citizen. I've spent twenty-two years being called a thing that made people smile at me and then ask me to speak to a manager.</p></div>
    </div>
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. COURTHOUSE — ROOM 214 — CONTINUOUS</h4>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">WARD</p><p class="dline">The court doesn't rule on whether a name is good. Only whether it's yours.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">It's mine.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">WARD</p><p class="dline">Is it your mother's?</p></div>
      <p class="action">A pause long enough that the stenographer's hands stop.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">Dolores was her mother's. It's not mine to keep and not mine to throw away. It's a coat in a closet.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">WARD</p><p class="dline">Are you going to wear the coat?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">I don't know yet.</p></div>
      <p class="action">Ward studies her for a long moment. Then she writes two words on the order and signs it.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">WARD</p><p class="dline">Granted. Publish it for three weeks and bring me the affidavit. And Miss Reyes — the court doesn't care which name you answer to. Everyone else will.</p></div>
    </div>

TITLE: THE OATH
MIN: 6
@@SLATE
Camera :: Handheld in a marble corridor, following Dolly out — the camera keeps her centred and the ceiling keeps going up out of frame.
Roll :: Digital, 3200 ISO, the corridor lit by tall windows and a century of dust.
Costume :: The same blazer. The same shirt. The same person, which is the joke.
Environment :: The courthouse corridor at noon: a bench, a drinking fountain, a woman with a file folder, a phone going off in someone's bag.
@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. COURTHOUSE — CORRIDOR — NOON</h4>
      <p class="action">Dolly comes out of Room 214 with a certified copy in her hand and stops in the middle of the corridor because her legs have decided to have a hearing of their own.</p>
      <p class="action">MARISOL REYES, fifty-four, is sitting on the bench. She has been there the whole time and did not come in.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">You were out here.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MARISOL</p><p class="dline">I was out here.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">DOLLY</p><p class="dline">For how long?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MARISOL</p><p class="dline">Since eight. I brought a book. I opened it once.</p></div>

    </div>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MARISOL</p><p class="dline">Dolores is going to be so disappointed. And she's going to be dead about it, which means she gets to be right forever.</p></div>
