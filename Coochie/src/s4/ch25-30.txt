TITLE: DUAL-PURPOSE
MIN: 8
@@SLATE

@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">INT. WEXLER HALL — ROOM 118 — MORNING</h4>
      <p class="action">Sokolov introduces her in nine words, which is a joke that only two people in the room get.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">SOKOLOV</p><p class="dline">Civic Reyes. Former student. Ran the assignment further than I intended.</p></div>
      <p class="action">Civic stands. She has a deck of twelve slides and does not open it.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">CIVIC</p><p class="dline">I changed my name in June of my senior year because I thought a name was a product decision. I was right about the mechanism and wrong about the price.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">CIVIC</p><p class="dline">Here's the number. Two hundred thousand people saw a billboard with my face on it. Eleven people bought a car.     </div>
