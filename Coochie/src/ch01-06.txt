TITLE: THE BACKYARD
MIN: 5
@@SLATE

Costume :: Maya in oversized vintage denim with a marker stain on the knee. Leo in a plain T-shirt he will wear in all thirty chapters.
Environment :: A sunlit suburban backyard: patchy lawn, a battered picnic table, a melting popsicle, a distant lawnmower.
@@SCENES

      <p class="action">MAYA, nineteen, sits cross-legged on a faded quilt with a spiral notebook on her knee. Beside her, LEO, nineteen, throws a bruised tennis ball straight up and catches it with a dull thwack. He has not missed in eleven years.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">We're not calling it a movie.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">What are we calling it?</p></div>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">You want a tuxedo.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">I want the tuxedo to be somebody else's problem. I want the name to be ours.</p></div>
      <p class="action">She writes something in the notebook. He does not look, which is a small act of love.</p>
    </div>
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">EXT. SUNLIT SUBURBAN BACKYARD — LATE AFTERNOON</h4>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="paren">(without looking over)</p><p class="dline">You're still stuck on the title.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">It's not just a title, Leo. It's a statement. A reclamation. You say it out loud in a quiet room and suddenly everyone forgets how to breathe.</p></div>

      <p class="action">She stands, brushes grass off her jeans, and looks at the orange horizon.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">If we're making this film, we don't whisper around the edges. We put the whole messy, awkward truth right on the marquee.</p></div>
      <p class="action">Wide: two silhouettes against a burning amber sky, and the streetlights coming on one by one, humming.</p>
    </div>

      <p class="action">Pages: a horse. A bridge. A woman with a sword. A boy falling off a roof. A banner that says TWENTY-FOUR.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">What's the two-four?</p></div>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">Of this. Of the thing we're doing. Thirty chapters each, minimum. Some of them will need more and we'll give them more, because a story that's cut to fit is a story that's lying about its size.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">That's seven hundred and twenty chapters.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">Seven hundred and twenty <em>minimum</em>.</p></div>
      <p class="action">Leo closes the notebook very slowly, like a man closing a door on a room with a fire in it.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">LEO</p><p class="dline">Okay. We start with one.</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">We start with one.</p></div>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">MAYA</p><p class="dline">I'm going to make it about two kids who make a movie about that, and get it wrong, and get it right, and have to show it to twenty-seven people in a church basement.</p></div>

    </div>
