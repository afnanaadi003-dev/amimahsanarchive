Environment :: Alcott Hall with the lights out; the radiator ticking; a bedsheet with a crease down the middle that becomes a road for the length of the film.

<div class="scene">

      <p class="action">On screen: the mill yard at four in the afternoon. Roof glass. Light coming down in shafts so solid you could put a ladder in them. Men walking in through the gate, not acting, not looking at the camera.</p>

TITLE: THE NIGHT SHIFT

Costume :: Walter's coat over his good shirt, cap on now. Tessa in her hoodie with the hood up.

@@SCENES
<div class="scene">
      <h4 class="slug">EXT. MILL SITE — NIGHT</h4>
      <p class="action">A flat concrete slab the size of a football field, weeds in the seams, frost on it. Walter stands at the edge with his hands in his pockets. Tessa has followed him four blocks without being invited.</p>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">TESSA</p><p class="dline">This is it?</p></div>
      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">WALTER</p><p class="dline">This is the loading dock. You're standing in the parking lot. The building went from here to the road and it was four floors and the top floor was all glass.</p></div>

      <div class="speech"><p class="cue">WALTER</p><p class="dline">It's a slab now. In 1985 there were two hundred and forty looms in there and when they all ran at once you could feel it in your fillings.</p></div>
