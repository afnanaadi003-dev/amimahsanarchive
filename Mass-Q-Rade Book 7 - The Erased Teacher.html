THE ERASED TEACHER

Amara Okonkwo & The Mass-Q-Rade Network

Written by Amim Ahsan (writing under the pen name Amimul Ahsan)

For twenty-four years Amara Okonkwo has taught the girls of Government Girls Secondary, Zamfara, without a single missed roll call, a single complaint, or a single salary paid on time. When Directorate Order 44 suspends the rural curriculum, converts her classroom into a regional grain store and relocates female educators to agricultural camps, she obeys — and then, that same night, teaches forty-five girls chemistry by moonlight. Three hundred kilometres above the Sahara, Mass-Q-Rade has been reading her silence as a ledger. Now the erased teacher holds the one weapon the ministry cannot confiscate: the truth, written where everyone can see it.
