About the atlas

Good books make the world bigger.

The Reading Atlas is an independent book discovery guide for curious readers. Explore fiction, philosophy, history, science, memoir, poetry and other influential works from different cultures and periods.

This is the reading room of GreenWorld: a hand-finished list of books on nature, science, history, politics, memory and human dignity. Every card shows the book's real cover, taken from its Open Library catalogue record, and the record link opens edition details, subjects and free lending copies where they exist.

GreenWorld provides short editorial descriptions, not unauthorized copies of copyrighted books. For reading access, borrow through Open Library or your local library, or buy from an authorized bookseller.
