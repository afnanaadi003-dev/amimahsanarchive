THE CAGED ARTIST

Kim Jong-soo · Pyongyang, 2016

Kim Jong-soo has spent twenty years painting the same smile on the same Leader for a state that calls it portraiture. Then a shipment of foreign pigment arrives with a broken tin of a colour that does not exist in his palette, and the brush he mixes it with begins to paint what he is actually looking at. Mass-Q-Rade does not offer him freedom. It offers him an audience of one, and a canvas that cannot be confiscated.
