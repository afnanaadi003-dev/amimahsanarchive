Lets Never Die

The Fibonacci of Dreams

Frankfurt, 1932. Elena Rothschild counts the keys of her grand piano — five black, eight white, thirteen in all — and believes mathematics is God's own language. Then the swastika begins its terrible subtraction: first her honour, then her home, then everyone she loves. What survives is not a girl but a cold-headed calculator of revenge — and a testimony the civilised world does not want to hear.
