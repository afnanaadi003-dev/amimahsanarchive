Nandini's Walk Home

by Amimul Ahsan

College vacation is over. Nandini is walking home. There is no one with her now. There are cars rushing on the road. It is a busy road. She is not walking at a standstill. I was able to recognize her from a distance. It is not difficult to recognize Nandini even in this darkness. She has her own style of walking. The wind has picked up. Her hair is swaying in the wind. Today Nandini has not tied her hair. She looks like a fairy with a mask on.
