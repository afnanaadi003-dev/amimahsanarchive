Shaon by the River

by Amimul Ahsan

We are walking along the riverbank. Me and Shaon. A song is playing on my mobile, Fairy Tale. A wonderful song by Warface. I don't know why I feel sad when I hear this song. What is that unknown reason? I have spent so many nights just listening to fairy tales. I wake up in the morning without sleep and go for a walk outside. The melody of the fairy tale is still playing, and this afternoon I am still feeling that feeling inside.
