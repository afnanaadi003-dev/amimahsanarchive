The Rules of the House

Hanse City

A city where every business is legal, or illegal, depending on which family signs the paper. Six names keep the lights on. One card turns them off.

The Deck 120

Cards, not chains

One hundred and twenty cards. Every player is dealt one hundred and fifty guti — a stake that cannot be traded, sold, or inherited. The deck has no owner card. The fifty-second is called The Eyes, and it is the only card that looks back.

The Litany 6

Jorda. Morda. Horda.

Corda, Torda, Forda. Six families, six keys. Markets, poker, cards, credit, screens, roads. Say the names and the city opens. Say them out of order and it closes on your hand. The litany is a prayer with a lock in it.

The Rule 1

Yours is yours

Every person has power over their own self, and no one else's. A woman is hers. A man is his. Nobody is anybody's pretty. The families call this rule bad for business. The city calls it the end of the game.
