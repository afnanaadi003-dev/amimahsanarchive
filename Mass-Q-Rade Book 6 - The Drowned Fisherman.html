THE DROWNED FISHERMAN

Miguel Santos & The Mass-Q-Rade Network

Written by Amim Ahsan (writing under the pen name Amimul Ahsan)

After twelve years of silent compliance paying illegal fines to the Aegis Marine Mining Consortium, Miguel Santos' world changes when a nanotech interface connects directly into his bloodstream. Gills form beneath his skin, his daughter Maya is cured of heavy-metal poisoning, and he becomes an apex entity in the deep — able to navigate toxic waters, see through darkness, and seed bio-corrosive bacteria that dissolve corporate infrastructure from within. From the suffocating reefs of Palawan to the sinking of the Aegis Citadel, Miguel's journey transforms him from broken fisherman into the ghost of the deep, striking at the heart of corporate impunity and ecological destruction.
