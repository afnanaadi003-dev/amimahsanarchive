THE FALLEN PRIEST

Father Michael O'Brien · County Tipperary, 1994

Father Michael O'Brien has heard eleven thousand confessions in a church with a leaking roof, and one of them was a crime. He has kept the seal for nine years, in a town where everyone already knows and nobody has said it. When a journalist arrives with a folder and a name, Mass-Q-Rade offers him the one thing a priest cannot manufacture: absolution that costs the sinner nothing and the witness everything.
