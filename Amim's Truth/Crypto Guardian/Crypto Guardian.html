Crypto Guardian

Crypto Guardian is the digital-economy desk of Amim's Truth, a collection of essays on philosophy, society, and human freedom by Amim Ahsan. It exists to make a notoriously noisy subject legible: what the market is doing, what the machinery underneath it is, and what a careful person should do about it.

We publish no price predictions and take no positions. Markets are described, never preached. Where the industry confuses speed with progress, we slow down and ask who carries the risk.

Data comes from the public CoinGecko API and refreshes every minute while the page stays open. If the feed is unavailable, the page says so plainly instead of inventing numbers.
