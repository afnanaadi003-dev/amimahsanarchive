THE EXACT MAN

Josiah Parsons Cooke · Harvard, 1884

He weighed the elements for thirty years and never once published a number he could not defend to a fifth decimal. Then, on a wet October night in the Harvard weighing room, his balance returns the same impossible digit for a sample that should not exist — and something answers from inside the instrument, offering him the one thing a careful man has never had: certainty, at no cost but doubt.
