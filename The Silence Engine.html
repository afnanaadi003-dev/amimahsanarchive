THE SILENCE ENGINE

Elias Vane · The Ninth Bell · Forty minutes

A forgotten acoustics paper, a machine that does not kill anyone, and a city that will thank the man who built it. Iris Kalu has to stop a campaign she helped make possible — without handing the city back to the order that sold her dying sister a relic.

Dramatis Personae

Nine People and a Bell

Click a chapter number to jump to where they arrive.

Iris Kalu

Acoustic forensics · 38

Wrote the paper that made the machine possible. Left the Order at nineteen. Her sister Nell died fourteen months after a revival tent sold her a fragment of the Bell.

Wants To be right, and to stop being right about the wrong thing.

Elias Vane

Acoustician · 44

Iris's former colleague and the co-author who took her name off the second paper. Calm, precise, entirely without the feeling he is trying to delete. He does not think of himself as a villain; that is the problem with him.

Wants A city that finally stops talking to itself.

Marek Kane

Ex-combat engineer · 36

Builds what he is told to build, then asks the question everyone else avoids. The child in Ward Nine is the crack that never closes.

Wants For the machine to have been for something.

Bishop Valerius

Keeper of the Ninth Bell · 67

Holy, and complicit. Kept an armory in a house of prayer and moved an accused brother through nine addresses instead of handing him to the police. He will confess to everything except the arithmetic that made him do it.

Wants For the Bell to hold the city up one more year.

Sister Aoife Brennan

Archivist · 31

Opens the door that ends the Order. She does not think that makes her brave; she thinks it makes her nine years late.

Wants For the files to be the justice they were supposed to be.

Det. Adjoa Osei

Homicide · 54

The only person in the film who keeps asking for evidence while everyone else is having a revelation. Gives Iris two days, twice.

Wants A name, an address, and a warrant that holds.

Dr. Halim Sarraf

Co-author · 63

The man who wrote the caveats that were torn out. One pencil note in a margin turns him from a coward into a witness, and the Order kills him for it.

Wants To have slowed him down by six months.

Sara Lindqvist

Hub technician · 28

Keeps the machine running and a private log in a swimming-pool locker. Deletes one honest line about a boy’s vitals and never stops thinking about it.

Wants To be able to tell her sister what she did.

Leo

Orientation cohort · 19

Can still hear a note nobody is playing. Volunteers to be the antenna because he has worked out exactly what he has left to spend.

Wants To be worth coming for.
