DHARWAD

The Cradle of Echoes

A young archivist steps off a state bus into the red-earth morning of Dharwad with a blank notebook and a borrowed recorder. In a college of basalt arches, a lake that keeps sound, and a radio shop that trades in old frequencies, he begins to suspect the town has been waiting for him to write something down.
