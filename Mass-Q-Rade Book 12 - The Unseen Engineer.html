THE UNSEEN ENGINEER

Yuki Tanaka · Yokohama, 2018

Four hundred and two hours of overtime in a year, one daughter who draws him as a grey rectangle, and a bearing defect he was told to pass. When Yuki Tanaka refuses to sign, Mass-Q-Rade offers him the perfect Japanese solution: to become a man with no record at all — no hours, no name, no weight on any ledger. Invisible, and therefore blameless.
