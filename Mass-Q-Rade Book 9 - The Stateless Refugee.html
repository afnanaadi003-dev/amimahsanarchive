THE STATELESS REFUGEE

Fatima Al-Hassan · Izmir & the Aegean, 2019

She left a country that no longer keeps a file on her and arrived in one that does not want to open one. When the registry asks Fatima Al-Hassan for a nationality, the room goes quiet, a broken radio begins to hum four beats and a pause, and something that calls itself Mass-Q-Rade offers her the only thing a border understands: weight. A country's worth of it, carried in her body, readable on any scale in Europe.
